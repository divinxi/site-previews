Reupholstery

We provide a full reupholstery service which can breathe new life into your old furniture.

You can select any from our range of thousands of fabrics and have your items reborn at our factory in no time at all. We can reupholster anything from the very small to the very large.

All of our reupholstery work is carried out at our own factory in Bristol by the same quality craftsmen that make our made to measure sofas. With our huge range of fabrics you can bring old furniture right up to date and create truly stunning individual pieces.

To find out more contact us or come along to our showroom in Clifton. Some images from the reupholstery process and finished examples are shown below.
