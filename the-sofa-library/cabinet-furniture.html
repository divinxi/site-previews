Cabinet furniture

Our cabinet furniture is made in the UK from sustainably sourced timbers. We have over 3000 pieces but can alter any piece to suit your needs

Our bespoke services are useful to get chests or wardrobes to fit alcoves or unusual ceiling heights.

We can make any piece in either pine, oak or mdf and can paint it any colour in the world.
