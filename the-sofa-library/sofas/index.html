Sofas to order

Our upholstery is hand built in our own factory in Bristol. We make everything from the frame upwards and can change any detail or dimension according to choice.

We make many variations on all our styles and have usually made every period style in the past.

We can change or adapt existing ranges or develop a special piece for you. We have the largest range of fabrics of any sofa retailer - you can put any fabric on any sofa.
