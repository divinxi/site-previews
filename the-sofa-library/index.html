Made to measure furniture

Everyday prices

About 50% of our orders for sofas and cabinet furniture are made to clients' own dimensions at very little additional cost.

We make upholstery and soft furnishings for many of the top hotels in London, including the Metropolitan on Park Lane and the Halkin in Belgravia. Our combination of excellent quality and keen price is combined with speedy delivery of 2-3 weeks.

We can design your new home, carry out the building work, and provide and install the furnishings and curtains.

German wardrobe systems

We supply brilliant quality German made wardrobe systems in about a calendar month that are around half the price of fitted alternatives.

Our range of German built wardrobes comes in over 30 door styles and hundreds of size and colour combinations. Choose something to suit you from standard sizes or made to measure, with a choice of sliding or hinged doors. An extensive range of modular wardrobe systems is on display in our Clifton store.
