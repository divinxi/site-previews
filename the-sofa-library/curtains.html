Made in our Bristol factory

Curtains to order

We make made to measure curtains at our own factory in Bristol from a range of thousands of fabrics.

We make curtains for many of London's top hotels and, whatever your requirements, we can create a look for you.

Hand made curtains

All of our curtains are hand-made to the highest standards. We can make all types of curtains with any heading, blackout or non blackout, and any other option you require.

Poles, tracks and fitting

We can also supply curtain poles or full tracking systems. We provide a full measuring and fitting service.

Blinds

We also make and supply all types of blinds, including roman and wooden blinds, and can advise on the best solution for your requirement.

Getting started

The best way to get started with our curtain making service is to come in to our store in Clifton, Bristol. Or you can email us your basic requirements, along with a rough measurement of the space the curtains will need to fill.
