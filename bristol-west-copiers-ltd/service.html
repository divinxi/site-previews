Service & support

Serviced by our own engineers

All equipment we supply is maintained by our own technical staff, trained by the manufacturer.

All equipment supplied by Bristol and West Copiers Ltd is serviced by our own technical staff, who are trained by the manufacturer. We are a management accredited company and commit our engineers to a maximum four-hour response to any breakdown of the equipment.

Service contracts

Following the installation of your photocopier, you will be offered a service contract if required, with a cost per page set according to your monthly volume. The cost per page includes everything needed for the reliable running of the equipment:

  • Service and maintenance
  • Parts
  • Labour
  • Toner
  • Consumables

Training and help after installation

On the day of installation, one of our engineers will give all members of staff full training. If you have any queries after installation, our Service Help Desk has in-house technicians you can contact at any time.
