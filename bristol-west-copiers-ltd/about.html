About us

A Bristol family business since 1980

One of Bristol's leading and longest-established photocopier providers.

Bristol and West Copiers Ltd is one of Bristol's leading and longest-established photocopier providers, formed in 1980. The company is a family business, created by Steven Bell, who remains the Managing Director today.

We specialise in the acclaimed Rex Rotary range of digital photocopiers, fax machines and multi-functional products, catering for a wide variety of businesses based primarily in the South West of England and South Wales.

Equipment that suits the way you work

We believe it is important to assess each individual customer's requirements, recommending a solution to enhance productivity and efficiency within a working environment.

The majority of businesses today need copying, printing, scanning and faxing to a varying degree. Where we can help is by making sure you have the most suitable equipment to guarantee a streamlined document workflow.
