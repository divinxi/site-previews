Rex Rotary copiers, multi-functional devices, fax and printers

Black and white and full colour photocopiers, ranging from low to high volume capacity.

What we supply

Over the years we have developed a close working relationship with the NRG Group, one of the largest distributors in the United Kingdom. The brand we supply is Rex Rotary. Our main speciality is black and white and full colour photocopiers, ranging from low to high volume capacity.

We also specialise in multi-functional devices configured with digital laser copying, printing, scanning and faxing. Alternatively, we stock a wide range of stand-alone fax machines and printers.

How we recommend a model

After a discussion with you, we will recommend a particular model. The recommendation depends on three main factors:

  1. Estimated monthly volume
  2. Type of documents being produced
  3. Features required

Ready to talk it through? Call sales on 0117 923 2333.

Features you might need

For example, a customer may need a combination of:

  • Duplex: double-sided copying
  • Electronic sorting and collating
  • Stapling
  • Hole punching
  • A5 to A3 paper size options
  • Separate bypass feed for copying onto card, adhesive labels and transparencies
  • Document feeder
  • Additional paper storage

Talk it through with us

Tell us your monthly volume, the documents you produce and the features you need, and we will recommend a model.
