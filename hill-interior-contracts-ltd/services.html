What we do

Project management, design and construction, refurbishment and building

Hill Interior Contracts Ltd can offer clients project management, design and construction, refurbishments or building services depending on their requirements.

How we run a project

We have established professional relationships with many highly qualified and experienced design and authority consultants, subcontractors and suppliers, whom we work with in the planning and delivery of our projects.

We operate with project focused structures that ensure our clients remain in direct personal contact with our senior management team throughout the duration of a project. This ensures clear, direct lines of communication and an end result that meets and exceeds our clients' expectations.

The main priority of Hill Interior Contracts Ltd will always be to provide a quality professional service to our clients.

Maintenance, call-outs and frameworks

Hill Interior also offer maintenance cover and fast response call-out repairs, and currently hold various framework agreements with local authorities. Works undertaken range from building extensions, office fit-outs and specialist internal and external refurbishments to Altro Whiterock, all types of floor coverings, painting and decorating, all aspects of partitioning, suspended ceilings, all types of joinery and external Rockpanel cladding.

For more information please contact us.
