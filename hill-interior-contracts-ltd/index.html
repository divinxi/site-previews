Quietly building a reputation

Hill Interior Contracts Ltd has been quietly building a reputation for internal refurbishment for many years. When we say quietly we actually mean quietly: one of our key areas of work is healthcare, where silent working is often necessary.

This degree of careful, thoughtful and disciplined working practice reflects everything that Hill Interior do well, and is applicable to many environments. We pride ourselves on delivering high quality commercial refurbishments, whilst maintaining long term partnerships through excellent customer service.

What we do

Hill Interior also offer maintenance cover, fast response call-out repairs and currently hold various framework agreements with local authorities.

Works undertaken include building extensions, office fit-outs and specialist internal and external refurbishments, Altro Whiterock, all types of floor coverings, painting and decorating, all aspects of partitioning, suspended ceilings, all types of joinery and external Rockpanel cladding.
