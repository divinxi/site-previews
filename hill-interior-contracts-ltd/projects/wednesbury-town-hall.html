Portfolio / Councils, libraries and heritage

Wednesbury Town Hall

Scope Refurbishment to create new offices, local housing services, councillors' chambers and public WC facilities

Building
Grade II listed
Client
Sandwell MBC

This project involved the refurbishment of the Grade II listed Town Hall at Wednesbury for Sandwell MBC. The project involved internal demolition works, new period windows, bespoke cash desk and reception counters, mechanical and electrical works, air conditioning, complete decorations, partitioning, suspended ceilings, vinyl floor coverings, Interface carpet tiles, Brintons carpets, Bushboard IPS and WC cubicles, automated door access, terrazzo floor repairs and cleaning of the existing quarry tiled floors.
