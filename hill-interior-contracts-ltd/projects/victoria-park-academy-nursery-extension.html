Portfolio / Schools and colleges

Victoria Park Academy – Nursery Extension

Client Sandwell Metropolitan Borough Council

This project involved the creation of a new nursery classroom at Victoria Park Academy. The structure was created using Metsec framing cloaked in a colourful Rockpanel facade. Internal works included new mechanical and electrical services, sprinkler system, WC IPS systems, suspended ceilings, floor coverings and decorations. The school was actively involved with the project: our site manager took part in the school assembly answering the children's questions, and the children voted on the three colours used to clad the exterior of the new extension.
