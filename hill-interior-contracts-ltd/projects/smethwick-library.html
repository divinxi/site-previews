Sandwell Metropolitan Borough Council – Smethwick Library

Role
Principal Contractor
Area
320 m²
Programme
8 weeks
Working height
5 m

Hill Interior was appointed Principal Contractor for Sandwell Metropolitan Borough Council's 320 m² refurbishment of the main lending library at Smethwick, Birmingham. The project started late in 2011 and first involved removing the existing concealed suspended ceiling system, which had been condemned in places. Working at a height of 5 m was not a problem for our PASMA trained employees, and once the ceiling had been removed Hill Interior installed a new high insulation suspended ceiling complete with a new lighting system.

Photos

The complete lending library was then redecorated with bright, engaging colours complementing the new fixtures and furniture layouts. High quality Milliken Dash carpet tiles were used to create a hard wearing environment while still giving comfort under foot for library patrons.

Working closely with library staff to keep the public updated was a big part of this contract, using signage, SMBC websites and Facebook. The contract was completed within the 8 week deadline, meeting the public relaunch date.
