Portfolio / Health and care

City Hospital Birmingham – Maternity Department

Scope Maternity department main corridors

Date
March 2010
Area
400 m²
Programme
12 weeks

In March 2010 Hill Interior undertook the total refurbishment of City Hospital's Maternity Department main corridor, combining products such as Thorlux Sky Dome, Thorlux Smart Control and Acrovyn hand and bump rails to provide a contemporary, practical and low maintenance working environment. Working closely with both client and architect, Hill Interior completed this 400 m² project within the 12 week schedule with no disruption to staff or patients.
