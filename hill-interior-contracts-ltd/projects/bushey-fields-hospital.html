Bushey Fields Hospital

Client Capital Projects – Kier Services East

Role
Interior fit-out supplier

Kier Services East appointed Hill Interior Contracts Ltd as their interior fit-out supplier for their Capital Projects contract at Bushey Fields Hospital. Hill Interior successfully undertook internal redecorations, vinyl floor coverings, anti-ligature solid grade IPS bathroom furniture, anti-ligature blinds and curtain tracks, institution mirrors, clinical and kitchen base units and worktops, Altro Whiterock hygiene wall panels and installation of smart anti-barricade doorsets.
