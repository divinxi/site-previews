Portfolio / Health and care

City Hospital Birmingham – Ward D5

Scope Refurbishment of ward sluice room

In April 2011 Hill Interior undertook the full refurbishment of City Hospital's Ward D5 sluice room. Combining modern materials and techniques, Hill Interior produced a durable and hygienic working environment. The walls were covered in Altro Whiterock and welded together, which with the vinyl floor covering provides a watertight work space. The ceiling has Armstrong Bioguard tiles and the wash hand basin uses Rada Acu sensor taps. Together with a stainless steel slop hopper and easy clean units, this makes an area with a low risk of cross infection.
