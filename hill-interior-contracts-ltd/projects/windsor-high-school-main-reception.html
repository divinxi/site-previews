Windsor High School – Main Reception

Scope Refurbishment of school main reception

Programme
2013 school holidays

During the 2013 school holidays, Hill Interior Contracts Ltd were commissioned on behalf of Windsor High School to carry out modernisation and refurbishment works to the main reception area.

The project started with lining the existing uneven walls and ceiling, creating the smooth modern look the school wanted. Complete redecoration, new lighting, internal fire doors, carpet tiles and a unique reception counter completed this visually vibrant reception area.

A bespoke solid surface wall was installed with the school crest routed into the reverse. The logo is lit from behind with LED lighting, showcasing it to all staff, pupils and visitors.
