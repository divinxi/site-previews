Worcester Museum and Art Gallery

Client Worcestershire County Council

Role
Principal Contractor
Building
Grade II* listed

Worcestershire County Council appointed Hill Interior as their Principal Contractor for the refurbishment of the Worcester Museum and Art Gallery. The project involved creating new office areas for the council to consolidate their resources into one centrally located building. With the building being Grade II* listed, close communication with both the Heritage Officer and architect was essential. The specification for the refurbishment included complete decorations with heritage paints, floor coverings, a new staff kitchen area, new staff WCs and shower room including Bushboard IPS and cubicles, mechanical, electrical and ventilation works, and bespoke sensitive hardwood skirtings, dado rails, doors and panelling.
