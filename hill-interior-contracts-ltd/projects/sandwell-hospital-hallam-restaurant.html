Portfolio / Health and care

Sandwell Hospital – Hallam Restaurant

Scope Public male and female WCs

Role
Principal Contractor
Client
Sandwell and West Birmingham Hospitals NHS Trust

Hill Interior was appointed Principal Contractor by Sandwell and West Birmingham Hospitals NHS Trust for the refurbishment of Sandwell Hospital's Hallam Restaurant public WCs. The project required modernising the existing layout of the male and female toilets using cutting edge materials and workmanship. The walls were covered in Altro Whiterock with welded joints, giving a totally sealed and hygienic wall solution. Polyflor's new Polysafe Modena PUR was used for the floor covering, providing a non slip floor with the easy clean properties of a traditional HD vinyl. Contrasting IPS panels and cubicles were combined with Rada Sense taps and waterless urinals to create a low maintenance and hard wearing space.
