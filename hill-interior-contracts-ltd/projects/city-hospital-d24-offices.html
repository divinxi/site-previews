Portfolio / Health and care

City Hospital – D24 Offices

Area
Approx. 450 m²
Role
Principal contractor
Result
Ahead of schedule, on budget

During 2013 Hill Interior acted as principal contractor for the refurbishment and modernisation of City Hospital's Ward D24. The brief was to create a flexible office environment for NHS staff to work in.

Photos

Ward D24 is approximately 450 m², including the main open plan office area, 3 toilets complete with IPS wall systems and DDA compliant facilities, kitchen, staffroom and meeting rooms.

Works undertaken were mechanical, electrical and refurbishment works including partitioning, suspended ceilings, floor finishes, decorations, Altro Whiterock and joinery works. Hill Interior Contracts Ltd completed the project ahead of schedule and on budget, allowing the client to take possession ahead of time.
