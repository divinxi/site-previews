Sandwell MBC – Council House

Scope 1st floor female WCs

This project involved the refurbishment of the existing female WCs and shower rooms on the 1st floor of the Council House. The existing WC was enlarged into the adjacent room, with the complete refurbishment of each room and new mechanical and electrical services to accommodate the additional sanitaryware. The council's specification included Bushboard IPS panels, cubicles and vanity units, new non slip floors, suspended ceilings and complete decorations.
