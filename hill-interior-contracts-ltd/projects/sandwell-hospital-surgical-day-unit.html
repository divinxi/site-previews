Sandwell Hospital – Surgical Day Unit

Scope Refurbishment of complete ward

Date
November 2010
Area
350 m²
Programme
6 weeks
Result
On time, on budget

Hill Interior undertook the refurbishment of Sandwell General Hospital's 350 m² Surgical Day Unit in November 2010. The project had a tight schedule of 6 weeks, and Hill Interior completed on time and on budget. As main contractor, Hill Interior worked closely with hospital management and sub-contractors, creating a modern, user friendly environment for staff and patients.
