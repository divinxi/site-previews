Hill Interior was appointed Principal Contractor for South Birmingham College's Hall Green Campus 2011 Summer Works Project. The project had a tight deadline, so all contracting companies needed to be programmed to allow a smooth continuity of work.

Photos

The project started with Hill Interior producing the Construction Phase Plan for the CDM Co-ordinator to approve. Once this was achieved, site meetings were held and Contractors' Information Packs were sent to all companies involved. Site inductions and toolbox talks were essential to this project because of the volume of contractors and the continually evolving environment.

While overseeing electricians, roofing contractors and civil engineers, Hill Interior had 2,500 m² of existing floor coverings, 5,500 m² of suspended ceilings, 7,000 m² of redecoration and 3,500 linear metres of partitioning to complete. The works started at the end of the academic term with a complete refurbishment of the two main college buildings, A and C blocks.

The programme ran for 10 weeks and, as always, health and safety was extremely important. Daily audits were carried out by Hill Interior's site foreman and weekly audits by WA Management, our external consultants, which let Hill Interior stay on top of potential problems and rectify existing ones. Towards the end of the contract staff and pupils were on site, so good co-operation, signage and barriers were a must, especially on enrolment days.

The project was completed to the college's high standards, providing a modern, functional and hard wearing environment for their students.
