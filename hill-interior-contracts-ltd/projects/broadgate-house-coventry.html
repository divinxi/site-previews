Broadgate House, Coventry

Client Coventry City Council

Floors
3 to 5

This project involved the internal refurbishment and fit-out of floors 3 to 5 of Coventry City Council's existing Broadgate House. Works included decorations, floor coverings, staff break-out areas, sound proofing to offices, M&E works, solar window film, fire doors and staff and disabled WC refurbishments.
