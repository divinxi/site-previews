George Salter Academy

Scope 6th Form boys' WC refurbishment

Programme
Christmas end of term

This project involved the refurbishment of George Salter Academy's 6th Form boys' WCs during the school's Christmas end of term. The existing WCs were stripped of their fixtures and fittings, then the existing entrance doorway was blocked up and a new, larger, full height entrance way created. M&E and ventilation services were adapted to suit the new layout. Walls received full height hygienic wall panels along with new full height WC cubicles, sanitaryware, vanity unit, floor coverings, suspended ceiling, LED lighting and decorations.
