Industrial fasteners · Halesowen, West Midlands · Since 1984

Spun galvanised nuts and bolts for transmission towers, substations, telecoms and rail gantries.

Holtbolt was founded in 1984 and has become a major supplier of industrial fasteners to industry, locally and nationally. Alongside the galvanised range we stock zinc plated, stainless steel and self colour nuts and bolts.

Who we supply

Since it began, Holtbolt has stocked spun galvanised fasteners for companies that make:
