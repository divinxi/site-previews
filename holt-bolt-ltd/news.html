News archive

Stories from Holtbolt, 2010 to 2014.

17 January 2014

Happy 30th birthday, Holtbolt

Established in 1984, Holtbolt celebrates its 30th anniversary in 2014.

Following her younger brother Jack, Emmy has recently joined the family business. Here’s to another 30 years!

16 January 2014

Holtbolt and the London Marathon

After Jack’s successful summit of Kilimanjaro last January, he has now signed up for the London Marathon.

On 13 April Jack and his good friend Matt Rose will be looking to complete the gruelling 26.2 miles around the streets of London.

Their training has already started, running through the wind, rain and flood waters of Worcester.

All proceeds raised will go to Primrose Hospice.

If you would like to donate, please follow the link: justgiving.com/Matthew-Rose5

13 January 2013

Inkberrow goes to Mount Kilimanjaro

To celebrate Jack Harris’ two-year anniversary of joining the company, he will be embarking on a journey to Africa to try to conquer Kilimanjaro.

After months of training, Jack departs on 18 January for a 10 day trek to raise money for the Everyman Cancer Charity.

You can donate at justgiving.com/inkberrow

10 April 2011

Jack Harris joins Holtbolt

Holtbolt is pleased to announce that Jack Harris has recently joined the company. Over the coming months Jack hopes to introduce himself to all our existing customers as well as developing potential new business.

25 August 2010

Gormley structure finished

Holtbolt has recently supplied a range of M16 to M30 spun galvanised bolts for the latest Antony Gormley sculpture, “Exposure”, which was fabricated in Scotland and shipped out to Lelystad, Holland, for erection on site.
