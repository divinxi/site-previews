Letting your property

We offer two types of letting service: let only, or full management.

Your decision depends on whether you wish to stay in charge of rent collection and inspections of your property, or leave the management of the let to the trusted team here at Blue.

Hassle-free option

Full Management Service

We take complete control of renting your property from beginning to end. In addition to everything in the let only service, we will:

  • Arrange the inventories
  • Collect the rent and pay it to you quickly, less our fees
  • Hold the tenant’s deposit
  • Inspect and report on your property quarterly
  • Advise you when the tenancy is coming to an end and seek your instructions for re-letting or selling
  • Arrange any dilapidations with tenants before releasing their deposit
  • Arrange any necessary cleaning and minor repairs, deducting from the deposit where applicable
You manage the let

Let Only Service

On receipt of your instructions to let your property, we will take care of:

Marketing
Circulating details of your property to potential clients, marketing it on the Rightmove website, advertising in the local press and placing a to let board at the property.
Viewings
Accompanying prospective tenants and advising you immediately of tenancy applications.
Vetting candidates
Interviewing, and obtaining credit references and guarantors when needed.
Tenancy agreement
Preparing the documentation required by current legislation, and collecting your first month’s rent and deposit.
