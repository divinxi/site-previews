Who we are

G Collier & Sons Ltd is a high quality electrical contractor of 30 years’ experience, located in the Knowsley Business Park area of Knowsley, Merseyside.

Our quality services and reputation have been built up on meeting demanding client requirements to support their business objectives.

We excel at providing emergency breakdown response to cover general electrical supply failure, repairs or the installation of whole electrical systems.

Our excellent call-out service operates on a 24/7 basis and will cater for project budgets from £50 to £500,000.00.

Customer needs and benefits

Less downtime, fewer disruptions

We are a modern company with a workforce that shares our vision of being at the forefront of providing a quality service to our customers. At crucial moments customers want a fast service with minimum disturbance and high efficiency to reduce production downtime.

We provide a first class service and want to see a partnership develop with customers, based on reliability and mutual understanding of achieving solutions to electrical problems that may affect their business activity.

We also offer a comprehensive design and build facility for electrical control systems, as a complementary service to support the general electrical maintenance and installation activities for the industrial and commercial sectors, backed by our 24/7 cover.
