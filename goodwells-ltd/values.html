Corporate values

Integrity, reliability, transparency, courtesy and trust: the common values we have reintroduced to our industry.

  1. 01Clear and Simple Terms

    Plain and simple documentation, from point of sale to hand-over. No hidden ‘small print’, easy to understand common language, and straightforward pricing. Need it be any other way? We don’t think so.

  2. 02Planning Ahead

    Understanding your desired outcome and working towards achieving it with the minimum of fuss. We’ve learned from our decades of experience, and appreciate that being sympathetic to your needs ensures the desired outcome. We always seek to get it right first time, every time.

  3. 03Adding Value

    Your home is your most important investment. So at all times, we seek to advise on the best solution to ensure your project will make a real contribution to the future value of your home. Our many happy clients have come to expect this kind of consideration as simply part of the service.

  4. 04Working in Harmony

    Builders working in harmony: whatever next? Our highly experienced Installation Teams work cohesively, not only with each other, but with you, our clients. We’re constantly mindful of the need to respect your home, and will keep you informed every step of the way to ensure the minimum of fuss and disruption. We guarantee it.

  5. 05The Way Forward

    We simply don’t see the need for directly employed salesmen. Our products sell themselves, and we’re firm believers in the ‘try before you buy’ concept. We insist our new customers either visit, or speak with the owners of, one of our fine installations before making a final decision. We believe this is THE way forward. Once again, we’re setting the standard others only seek to get close to.

  6. 06Attention to Detail

    ‘The answer’s in the detail’, so the saying goes. We fully appreciate this, and continually strive to get it right. This is not something we fix along the way: from the outset we plan to achieve only the best of results for you. It’s the finishing touches that make us truly stand out from the crowd.

  7. 07Peace of Mind

    We don’t believe in leaving anything to chance, so you can rest assured that your dedicated Project Manager ensures our rigorous Quality Assurance systems are followed to the letter. Our high quality workmanship promotes our work long after we have completed your project.

  8. 08Assured

    There’s a fine line between confidence and arrogance. We’re proud to be confident, and believe this has real value. A builder you can depend upon is in itself a rare thing; one who stakes their reputation on such assurances on a daily basis is something of real value.

  9. 09Within Reach

    With the right guidance you might be surprised at what you can achieve. With our flexible approach to budget-sensitive specifications, excellent purchasing power with our suppliers and partners, and a desire to nurture long-lasting relationships, we can help you maximise your investment.

  10. 10Exceeding Expectations

    Ever been left with a feeling of disappointment where, just like that old school report, the words ‘could do better’ best describe the service you’ve received? We recognise the importance of recommendation to the future of our business, and statements of this kind just don’t sit well. We say ‘It doesn’t have to be that way’, and our customers are inclined to agree. So why tolerate the rest? Better is good, and we believe in better.
