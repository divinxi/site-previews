Adapted Living Solutions

Whatever the reason, bespoke adaptations to cater for all needs in all circumstances.

Do you require bespoke or adapted installations? We are highly experienced and fully able to provide solutions to cater for all needs and circumstances. Whichever adaptation you are looking for, we guarantee the workmanship, quality and attention to detail will exceed your expectations.

Extension adaptations

  • Bedroom and en-suite
  • Bathroom
  • Kitchen-diner

The need for additional space, to create a more suitable and comfortable environment on a ground floor, is readily achieved by adding an extra room. This new space can be used as a bedroom and en-suite, a bathroom, or a combined space such as a kitchen-diner. Whatever the need, this cost-efficient route can open up previously unexplored options, making your home fit your day-to-day requirements while letting you continue to enjoy your home safely and conveniently, particularly when in a shared occupancy situation.

Enquire about extension adaptations

Kitchen adaptations

  • Side-opening ovens
  • Rise and fall work surfaces
  • Rise and fall sink units and hobs
  • Innovative storage

Traditional kitchen layouts do not always consider the needs of those with reduced mobility and can be potentially hazardous environments. With an experienced eye, modifications can be simple, straightforward, and more affordable than expected. Side-opening ovens, rise and fall work surfaces, sink units and hobs, and innovative storage options can transform the way your kitchen works, with you in mind.

Enquire about kitchen adaptations

Bathroom adaptations

  • Walk-in baths
  • Level-access showers
  • Wet rooms
  • Automated lifting and lowering devices
  • Rise and fall basins and WCs

There is an incredibly diverse range of bathroom fittings specifically designed to bring peace of mind and independence to your home when a traditional bathroom just isn’t suitable. Walk-in baths, level-access showers, wet rooms, fully automated lifting and lowering devices, rise and fall basins and WCs: whatever the requirements, Goodwells has the experience and the knowledge to help you create the ideal environment without compromising on style.

Enquire about bathroom adaptations

Driveway & landscaping adaptations

  • Ease of entry
  • Safe manoeuvring
  • Outdoor to indoor transition

Adaptations are not just limited to the house. Ease of entry and safe manoeuvring are equally important to the enjoyment of the home and continued independence. Our extensive experience in these often overlooked areas allows us to create a sympathetic yet unobtrusive transition from outdoors to in.

Enquire about driveway & landscaping adaptations

Whole house conversions

  • Ramps and rails
  • Door-widening
  • High-level plug sockets

Most of us wish to continue to live in our own homes for as long as possible, but many homes are just not designed to meet changing needs as we age. In most cases, however, there are many small yet significant adaptations that can make a home a much safer and more comfortable place to be. From ramps and rails to door-widening and high-level plug sockets, almost every style of home can be made safer and more convenient to live in. Working in partnership with you and your Occupational Therapist, we can tailor your home to suit your needs.
