Spectacles and sunglasses

Frames, lenses and fitting advice from our dispensing team. Looking for sunglasses? Any of our frames can be tinted.

  • Dispensing team led by our dispensing optician
  • Thinner, lighter lenses
  • Tailor made progressive lenses
  • Modern, stylish and designer frames
  • Advice on the frame to suit your face shape
  • Any frame can be tinted as sunglasses

At Freemans our dispensing team, led by our dispensing optician, is fully qualified and trained to give the best advice on your spectacles.

We can advise on the best lenses for your prescription, from thinner, lighter lenses to tailor made progressive lenses.

We have a wide variety of modern and stylish frames, including many designer frames, and can advise on the best frame to suit your face shape. Please contact us for more information.
