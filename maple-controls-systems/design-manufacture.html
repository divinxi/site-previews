Building Management Controls

Full installs

Maple will provide full BMS, EMS and BEMS to suit every need and eventuality that you are dealing with.

Systems updated

We can provide a service to update existing systems to current standards and needs, or to provide a set up that will cater for the future needs you know about and some that you may not!

Door access

We can incorporate security measures to door access, and control and monitor them through BMS systems.

Lighting control

Lighting is often overlooked in terms of controllability. Let us show you how we can make your premises more energy efficient, safer and a better environment to be in.

Custom written software

Software for existing systems

No matter what you need, Maple can write software to suit your exact requirements and adapt this with you into the future.

Business cases developed

Even systems that are considered old can still be very effective if the software is up to date and current to what you need. We can update and refresh all settings to suit, and help keep capital costs down, allowing more time and budget for the controlling equipment.

Custom build of hardware

Bespoke builds of panels & control modules

We personally build all control panels and interface units to match what you have asked for. We build every single panel from the ground up, so know every aspect and component in it. We can provide anything you need to measure, monitor or control any individual need you have.

Existing hardware modified

Maple can in many instances take existing hardware and bring it up to date to suit current requirements. We can do this across a range of systems and components, and ensure that they all communicate and act as a fully integrated system.

Design & Manufacture

Task specific design service

We not only manufacture and install controls and systems, but we fully design them too. We are very well experienced in all types of mechanical and electrical installs, which helps us provide a system that can do what you want it to. This understanding, from the client’s wish to full execution, makes our knowledge unrivalled.

Off-site modular manufacture

As we know what is needed and design it ourselves, much of the hardware is manufactured off site and brought to you in modular ‘plug n play’ form. This means that time on site is kept to a minimum and quality standards are kept high, as a full range of testing is undertaken before any equipment leaves us.
