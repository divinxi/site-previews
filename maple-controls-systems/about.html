Delta, Honeywell, Sauter

Maple is partnered to provide systems from Delta, Honeywell and Sauter and can provide the extensive service provision from each system that we select to best suit your needs, not just what kit fits the best.

Schneider (maintain only)

We have taken the decision to only supply the above systems due to their adaptability, but can also provide a full maintenance and update service for Schneider software and systems.

Coverage

UK wide (not call out)

We work all over and welcome projects in all parts of the UK.

Response times

We can arrange for online response 24-7, and, location dependent, even a 2 hour call out to arrive on site.

Assistance & back up

The vast majority of clients don’t need a 24-7 service, but every client needs to know as soon as anything happens that may interrupt normal service or production. We provide this by offering several levels of support:

  • simple SMS or email notifications
  • phone contact
  • online diagnostics
  • full site visit call out

Again, you can choose what suits you and can change service provision at any time.
