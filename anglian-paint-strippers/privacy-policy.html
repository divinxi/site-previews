Privacy policy & GDPR

This privacy policy and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) statement is designed to demonstrate our firm and continuing commitment to the privacy of personal information provided by those visiting and interacting with this website. We hold the privacy of your personal information in the highest regard. The following discloses our information gathering and dissemination practices for this website.

We recognise the importance of protecting your privacy and our policy is designed to assist you in understanding how we collect, use and safeguard the personal information you provide to us and to assist you in making informed decisions when using our site. This policy will be continuously assessed against new technologies, business practices and our customers' needs.

Who we are

Mitchell Reclaim Ltd is a company incorporated and registered in England & Wales under the company number 11248360. Our registered company address is 2 Anson Road, Norwich Airport Industrial Estate, Norwich, Norfolk NR6 6ED.

What personal data do we collect?

Mitchell Reclaim Ltd collects different types of personal data including your name, address, telephone number and email address. This information can be collected via phone, email and our website. As such we have a duty of care and obligation under the law to treat the data we collect with care, and it will be kept confidential. We will comply with our obligations and safeguard your rights under the GDPR at all times.

How do we use your data?

Mitchell Reclaim Ltd will process the information you provide us in the manner compatible with the GDPR at all times. We will endeavour to keep your information accurate and up to date and not keep it for longer than is necessary or in accordance with the data law. The time frame of keeping the data could be governed by specific business-sector requirements and agreed practices. We collect and process your personal data either because it is necessary for our performance of a contract with you or replying to emails from you and comments left on projects and gathering feedback to enable us to continually improve our service.

You will have the right to withdraw your consent to us using your personal data at any time, and to request we delete it.

Security of data

Data security is very important to us and to protect your data we have taken suitable measures to safeguard and secure data collected.

We will use technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data. Along with these we have enforced new policies and procedures to further protect the data, including GDPR training for current employees and an induction program for new employees, so protecting your data stays a priority.

Do we share your data?

In certain circumstances, we may be legally required to share certain data held by us, which can include personal data, for example if we were involved in legal proceedings, where we are complying with legal obligations, a court order, or government authority.

We may also sometimes contact third parties to supply services to you on our behalf to fulfil an agreed contract. These may include payment processing, delivery of goods and services. Where any of your data is required for such purpose, we will take all reasonable steps to ensure that your data will be handled safely, securely and in accordance with your rights, our obligations, and the obligations of the third party under law.

If you think we have incorrect information or you would like a copy of the information we hold about you

If you believe that the information we are holding on you is incorrect or incomplete, please write or email us as soon as possible at the address below. We will promptly correct the data which is found to be incorrect.
